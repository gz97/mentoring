# TASK: Read up on business data models

## Overview
- one data model does not fit-all. Models are blueprints for a specific context, and a business data model provides framework for data structure for a specific business problem
## Books to read:
- Data Modeling Made Simple: A Practical Guide for Business & IT Professionals (2009) - Steve Hoberman
- Data Model Patterns: Conventions of Thought (2011) - David Hay
- The Data Model Resource Book, Volume 1: A Library of Universal Data Models for All Enterprises (2001) - Len Silverston
- The Data Warehouse Toolkit: The Definitive Guide to Dimensional Modeling (2013) - Ralph Kimball and Margy Ross
- Data Modeling Essentials, Third Edition (2004) - Graeme Simsion and Graham Witt
